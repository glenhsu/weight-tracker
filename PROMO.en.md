## Long (for blog / Facebook / Reddit)

**Why is your gym data in someone else's hands?**

Every fitness app wants:
- Your email ✅ Now they have your identity
- Your body stats ✅ Now they know your fitness level
- A subscription ✅ Monthly fees forever
- Your attention ✅ Ads mid-workout

**Solo Level Up V2.0** takes a different path:

**One HTML file. Zero backend. Zero uploads. Zero trackers.**

Your weight, reps, sets, PRs — everything stays in your phone's localStorage. Even the developer can't see it.

▎Features:
- Workout templates (exercise name, reps, weight in separate fields)
- Start a workout (follow your template, tap sets done)
- Rest timer (beeps at 3s, rings when time's up)
- PR leaderboard (auto-calculated 1RM, synced when workouts are deleted)
- Full history
- Dark theme, mobile-first

▎Getting started:
1. Open index.html on your phone
2. Create Push / Pull / Legs templates
3. Add exercises: Bench Press 60kg × 10
4. Start training 👊

**iOS**: Safari "Add to Home Screen" → standalone fullscreen app  
**Cross-device**: Drop the HTML in iCloud / Google Drive → access anywhere

🔗 GitHub: https://github.com/glenhsu/weight-tracker
